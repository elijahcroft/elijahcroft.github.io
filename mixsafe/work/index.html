MixSafe — selected work

The best of
the record.

108 pieces from the full MixSafe design record: the web layouts that shipped, every sticker that scored seven or better, and the finished print, social and logo work.

01 / The checker page

Designing the answer, not the form

Three full visual directions for mixsafe.org, pitched on the same four-substance query so the comparison is design, not content. Then the layouts that survived in-person testing: one field that takes a whole mix in a single line, and two result-card layouts that went live.

02 / Stickers

The ones that earned a print run

Every sticker round was rated one to ten before anything went to a printer. These 37 are the pieces that scored seven or better — strips, die-cuts, a pharmacy vial, a receipt, and a census where every dot is a real rated pair.

03 / Campus flyers

One claim per sheet

The full flyer series. Each poster makes exactly one argument and backs it with a figure from the tool's own data, with its own QR code so every sheet can be measured on its own.

04 / Low-ink print & the check kit

Built to survive a campus printer

Light-ground versions for a library printer and a club officer's budget, approval-safe pieces for facilities review, and the card and pouch label for test-strip kits handed out through campus health programmes.

05 / Social & promo

The same system, sized for a feed

Carousel sets for Instagram and Threads, where every rating shown is checked against the app's own lookup, plus the digital promo kit: feed post, story, link card and key visual.

06 / Logo

The information pill

The capsule split across its seam, and the wordmark and alternates developed around it.
